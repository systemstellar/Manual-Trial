Performance reviews

Your performance review is an opportunity to reflect on your achievements, discuss your development goals, and explore future career opportunities within Stellar.

Career progression is assessed on demonstrated capability, performance, values alignment, the impact and contribution you make to the business, and your readiness for broader responsibility - alongside whether the business has a genuine need for that role at the time.

Review cycle

Your first appraisal is timed from your start date. After that, you move onto one of two annual review dates - 1 July or 1 January - based on your start-date cohort.

1 January – 30 June startersToday falls here

  • First annual appraisal in May/June of the following year
  • Thereafter, align with the 1 July – EOFY review cycle each year

1 July – 31 December startersToday falls here

  • First annual appraisal in November/December of the following year
  • Thereafter, align with the 1 January – End-of-Year review cycle each year

You get one annual review - which of these two dates it falls on, 1 July (end of the Australian financial year) or 1 January (the mid-year point), depends on your start-date cohort. Your annual salary review is aligned to the same date.

Career progression

  • One formal appraisal per year, on the cycle set by your start date
  • Career progression is based on readiness, not tenure alone
  • For specific seats like Credit Coach, Team Leader and Team Manager, timing also depends on the business having a genuine opening - being ready simply means you're well placed whenever that opportunity comes along
  • Salary and career discussions form part of the annual review process

The Credit Stellar Compass

This traces one common path: Credit Associate → Senior Credit Associate → Credit Analyst → Senior Credit Analyst, then often either Credit Coach → Credit Team Leader → Credit Team Manager, or a pivot into Learning & Development (Senior Associate → Manager). Credit Team Leader can also lead into Loan Processing Manager, its own technical leadership track. Everything past Senior Credit Analyst is shown as its own star on the map simply because these are genuine, reachable destinations - not a single fixed ladder everyone is expected to climb the same way.
